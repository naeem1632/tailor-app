Report Calculations:
Total Earned: Sum of all work value in payment records for the period
Advances Given: Total advance payments given to employee in the period
Amount Paid: Total amount credited to employee via payment records
Advance Deducted: Advances deducted from payments (recoveries)
Actual Cash Paid: Real cash/transfer given = Amount Paid - Advance Deducted
Balance: Total Earned - Advances Given - Actual Cash Paid
Formula Breakdown:
Total Cash Out = Advances Given + Actual Cash Paid
Balance = Total Earned - Total Cash Out
PENDING = Balance > 0 (Money owed to employee)
SETTLED = Balance = 0 (Fully settled)
OVERPAID = Balance < 0 (Paid more than earned)
